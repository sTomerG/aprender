# Aprender

Spaanse werkwoorden stampen via invuloefeningen, met focus op persoonsvormen.

## Language

**Exercise**:
One practice unit: a single Spanish verb whose infinitive and person forms the learner must fill in and submit together.
_Avoid_: Card, quiz, lesson, session

**Infinitive**:
The unconjugated dictionary form of the Spanish verb; in an Exercise it is itself a fill-in, cued by its Dutch meaning.
_Avoid_: Base form, stem, dictionary form

**Person form**:
A conjugated verb form for one of the six grammatical persons always present in an Exercise: yo, tú, él/ella/usted, nosotros, vosotros, ellos/ellas/ustedes — each cued by its person label.
_Avoid_: Conjugation (alone), ending, answer row

**Gloss**:
The Dutch meaning shown as the cue for the Infinitive fill-in. Direction of practice is Dutch → Spanish.
_Avoid_: Translation, prompt

**Hint**:
A per-row control that reveals the correct answer for that fill-in without inserting it; the learner must still type the answer themselves.
_Avoid_: Reveal, cheat, autofill, solution button
